1. Information we collect

We only collect information that is needed to provide the app's features. You provide most of it directly when you register and use the app.

Account & profile information

  • Full name — used as your display name in the app.
  • Email address — used as your account identity, to sign you in, to verify your account, and to send password-reset and essential service emails.
  • Password — never stored in readable form; only a secure one-way hash is kept (see Storage & security).
  • Phone number — optional. If you provide it, it is stored but is not verified and is not used to contact you at launch.
  • Date of birth — used to confirm you meet the minimum age requirement (13+).
  • Security questions and answers — used to verify your identity for sensitive actions such as changing your password. Your answers are never stored or transmitted back in readable form; only a secure hash is kept.

Financial information you enter

  • Monthly income / salary and your preferred display currency.
  • Savings goal.
  • Expense entries you add — amount, category, an optional label, and the date.
  • Month-end summaries derived from the above.
We do not connect to your bank. HisaabWise has no bank linking or open-banking integration. All financial figures are ones you type in yourself. We never see your bank account, card numbers, or transactions unless you manually enter an amount.

Learning activity

  • Your progress through lessons, quiz results, experience points (XP), and your daily learning streak.

Technical & device information

  • Time zone — read from your device so that day boundaries, streaks, and month-end reports are calculated correctly for where you are.
  • Preferred language.
  • Push-notification token — if you enable notifications, so we can send reminders you have opted into.
  • Basic in-app usage events — anonymous, first-party product-usage events (for example, which screens are opened) that help us understand how the app is used and improve it.

2. What we do not collect

To be clear about the boundaries:

  • No access to your bank accounts, cards, or financial institutions.
  • No location data, camera, photo library, microphone, or contacts.
  • No third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, and no advertising identifiers. The app does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • No sale of your personal data — ever.
  • No in-app purchases, subscriptions, or payment collection. The app is free.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To provide the app's core features — budgeting, expense tracking, savings goals, and the month-end report.
  • To deliver the financial-education lessons and track your progress.
  • To send essential service messages (email verification, password reset) and any reminders you opt into.
  • To calculate dates, streaks, and reports correctly in your time zone.
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse, and diagnose problems.
  • To understand aggregate usage and improve the app.

4. Legal basis & consent

HisaabWise is offered in the United Arab Emirates and is designed to comply with the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021, "PDPL"). We process your personal data on the basis of:

  • Your consent, which you give when you accept this policy at registration; and
  • Performance of the service you have asked us to provide — we cannot run your account or the app's features without the information described above.

You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account (see Your privacy rights).

5. How we share information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We share it only in these limited situations:

  • Service providers. We use trusted infrastructure providers to host the app's backend and database. They process data only on our instructions and only to run the service. Our current providers include our application hosting platform and a managed cloud database (MongoDB Atlas).
  • Legal requirements. We may disclose information if required by applicable law or a valid legal request.
  • Protecting the service. To detect, prevent, or address fraud, security, or technical issues.

6. Storage & security

  • Your data is stored on secured servers operated by our hosting and database providers.
  • All data transmitted between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Passwords and security-question answers are protected with a strong one-way hash and are never stored or returned in readable form.
  • Access tokens are short-lived, and your refresh token is held securely in the device Keychain.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable and appropriate measures to protect your information.

7. Retention & deletion

We keep your personal data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account:

  • Your account is deactivated immediately and sign-in is blocked.
  • A 30-day grace period follows, during which you may recover the account.
  • After 30 days, your personal data is permanently erased.

Some records may be retained longer only where required to comply with legal obligations.

8. Your privacy rights

Under the UAE PDPL, you have the right to:

  • Access & portability — request a copy of your data. You can export your data from within the app.
  • Correction — update your profile details in the app at any time.
  • Erasure — delete your account and personal data (see above).
  • Withdraw consent — by deleting your account.

To exercise any right, use the in-app controls or contact us at the address below.

9. Children's privacy

HisaabWise is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. International transfers

Your data may be processed and stored on servers located outside the United Arab Emirates by our service providers. Where this happens, we take steps to ensure your data receives an appropriate level of protection consistent with the PDPL.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app. Continued use of HisaabWise after an update means you accept the revised policy.
